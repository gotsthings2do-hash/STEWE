# Stewart Enterprise Token (STEWE) — Project Whitepaper
**Version 1.0 · November 2, 2026**

## 1. Executive summary
Stewart Enterprise Token (STEWE) is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token associated with Stewart’s Enterprise LLC, created as a collectible/community token. Potential future utility may involve customer rewards, promotions, special products or experiences, and community participation. These possibilities are not active or guaranteed unless separately implemented.
